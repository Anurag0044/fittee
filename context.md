# FITTEE — BRAND & DESIGN CONTEXT

## THE BRAND

Fittee is a **tribal and regional clothing brand rooted in Jharkhand**.

It takes inspiration from:

- Tribal art
- Jharkhand's people
- Regional landscapes
- Folk traditions
- Local visual language
- Indigenous stories
- Nature
- Regional identity

and translates them into **contemporary clothing**.

Fittee is not a corporate apparel company.

It is not primarily a customization business.

It is not a generic print-on-demand store.

It is a **fashion brand built around cultural identity**.

---

# THE CORE IDEA

## WEAR YOUR CULTURE WITH PRIDE.

Fittee exists to make regional culture wearable.

The philosophy is:

> **Culture should not live only in history books, museums, or traditional artifacts. It can live in what we wear today.**

The clothing should feel contemporary enough for an urban audience while remaining deeply connected to Jharkhand.

---

# THE CENTRAL TENSION

The entire visual identity should be built around:

## ROOTED IN JHARKHAND × MADE FOR TODAY

or:

## TRIBAL ROOTS × MODERN WEAR

This is the key creative tension.

### RURAL / REGIONAL

Jharkhand  
Forests  
Hills  
Mud walls  
Tribal murals  
Folk art  
Handmade textures  
Earth  
Sunlight  
Community  
Nature

×

### URBAN / CONTEMPORARY

Oversized silhouettes  
Modern fashion  
Streetwear  
Editorial photography  
Minimal typography  
Clean interfaces  
Contemporary styling  
Premium presentation

The website should make these two worlds feel naturally connected.

---

# WHAT FITTEE SHOULD FEEL LIKE

When someone enters the website:

### First reaction

**“This is beautiful.”**

Then:

**“This feels different.”**

Then:

**“This is Jharkhand.”**

Then:

**“I want to wear this.”**

The website should feel like entering a **fashion world**, not browsing a business website.

---

# POSITIONING

Fittee should sit somewhere between:

### CONTEMPORARY FASHION

and

### CULTURAL STORYTELLING.

The brand should feel capable of standing beside modern Indian fashion labels while having a visual identity that is unmistakably its own.

The cultural identity should be a **strength**, not a decorative theme.

---

# VERY IMPORTANT: DO NOT MAKE IT LOOK “TRIBAL-THEMED”

Avoid the obvious interpretation:

- Tribal patterns everywhere
- Excessive ethnic decoration
- Random indigenous symbols
- Tourist-shop aesthetic
- Museum aesthetic
- Excessive brown/orange
- Heavy traditional borders
- Cultural motifs pasted onto generic UI

Instead:

Use culture through:

**Photography**

**Artwork**

**Typography**

**Material**

**Environment**

**Storytelling**

**Product design**

**Composition**

The culture should feel **lived**, not manufactured.

---

# HERO

The hero should feel like a **fashion campaign**.

Not:

> ecommerce banner + headline + buttons.

Instead:

A cinematic scene from Jharkhand.

Real environment.

Real people.

Beautiful styling.

Fittee garments clearly visible.

Tribal artwork integrated into the clothing.

The environment should tell part of the story.

The headline can be:

# WEAR  
# YOUR  
# CULTURE  
# WITH PRIDE.

with **Culture** receiving the signature mustard/gold treatment.

The hero should communicate three things immediately:

### PLACE

Jharkhand.

### CULTURE

Tribal identity.

### FASHION

Contemporary clothing.

---

# PRODUCTS ARE THE PRIMARY COMMERCIAL HERO

Fittee is a clothing brand.

Therefore the homepage must showcase clothing beautifully.

The products should not be buried underneath cultural storytelling.

The visitor should quickly see:

- T-shirts
- Oversized T-shirts
- New arrivals
- Cultural collections
- Bestsellers

The product photography should feel like fashion editorial photography.

Not catalogue photography.

Not supplier photography.

Not generic mockups.

---

# PRODUCT PRESENTATION

Products should feel **desirable before informative**.

Instead of large ecommerce cards filled with UI:

Use large photography.

Minimal metadata.

Example:

### SOHRAI SPIRIT

Regular Fit Tee

₹699

○ ○ ○

The photograph should occupy most of the visual weight.

Avoid:

- giant badges
- floating cart buttons
- excessive borders
- heavy shadows
- giant pills
- excessive icons
- overly rounded cards

The product should feel like it belongs in a fashion editorial.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Use a strong editorial typography system.

### DISPLAY

Refined serif.

For:

- Hero
- Collection names
- Brand statements
- Cultural stories

### UI

Clean contemporary sans-serif.

For:

- Navigation
- Product details
- Prices
- Filters
- Buttons

### HANDWRITTEN

Extremely limited.

Used for occasional cultural annotations such as:

> People  
> Roots  
> Stories  
> On Fabric

The handwritten style should feel like a designer's annotation—not a decorative font used everywhere.

---

# COLOR

Primary palette:

### BONE / WARM CREAM

The primary canvas.

### CHARCOAL / BLACK

Typography and strong visual sections.

### DEEP OLIVE

Connection to nature and landscape.

### EARTH / CLAY

Regional warmth.

### MUTED MUSTARD

Fittee's signature accent.

Mustard should be **rare and intentional**.

It should highlight:

- important words
- selected states
- key CTAs
- artwork
- small graphic moments

Do not make the whole website yellow.

---

# CULTURAL VISUAL LANGUAGE

The visual system should derive from actual Jharkhand-inspired visual language.

Use:

- Sohrai-inspired forms
- regional linework
- natural motifs
- animals
- human figures
- landscapes
- geometric marks
- handmade textures

But keep them restrained.

A motif appearing once in the right place is more powerful than twenty motifs everywhere.

---

# PHOTOGRAPHY

Photography should be one of Fittee's biggest differentiators.

Show clothing in environments that feel connected to the region:

- forests
- hills
- villages
- textured walls
- stone
- fields
- warm sunlight
- cultural artwork
- natural landscapes

But style the models like a **modern fashion editorial**.

This creates:

## RURAL ENVIRONMENT × URBAN FASHION

That contrast is the Fittee signature.

---

# HOMEPAGE PHILOSOPHY

Do not build a repetitive ecommerce template.

Avoid:

Hero → card grid → card grid → card grid → generic story → footer.

Instead create a **visual journey**.

### 01

## THE CAMPAIGN

Hero.

### 02

## THE COLLECTION

Products.

### 03

## THE FIT

Regular vs Oversized.

### 04

## THE CULTURE

The story behind the artwork.

### 05

## THE LAND

Landscape / people / environment.

### 06

## THE ART

How traditional visual language becomes contemporary graphics.

### 07

## THE FABRIC

Tactile material experience.

### 08

## THE COMMUNITY

People actually wearing Fittee.

### 09

## THE BRAND

Why Fittee exists.

Then:

### SHOP.

The homepage should make the visitor **feel the brand before asking them to buy.**

---

# THE “WOW” MOMENT

Fittee needs one unforgettable interaction.

For example:

A traditional artwork appears as a hand-painted mark.

The camera moves through it.

The artwork transforms into the graphic printed on the garment.

The camera pulls back.

A person is wearing the finished T-shirt.

Then:

> **FROM OUR LAND.  
> INTO YOUR EVERYDAY.**

This is much more meaningful than adding random animations.

---

# CUSTOMIZATION

Customization exists, but it is **not the identity of Fittee**.

It should appear later in the experience.

It should never compete with:

- Collections
- Products
- Culture
- Brand story

The primary CTA hierarchy should be:

### 1. SHOP COLLECTION

### 2. EXPLORE COLLECTIONS

### 3. DISCOVER OUR STORY

Then customization can appear as a secondary pathway.

---

# NAVIGATION

Keep it fashion-focused:

**Shop**

**Collections**

**Our Story**

**Journal**

Then:

Search  
Account  
Bag

Customization can live inside Shop or as a secondary navigation item.

The navigation should not make Fittee look like an apparel manufacturer.

+

---

# THE FINAL DESIGN TEST

Look at the homepage with all text removed.

If the viewer can still feel:

**Jharkhand**

**culture**

**fashion**

**Fittee**

then the visual identity is working.

If it looks like a generic fashion website with tribal graphics added on top:

**FAIL.**

---

# ONE-LINE CREATIVE BRIEF

> **Build Fittee as a contemporary fashion brand born from Jharkhand—where tribal heritage, regional identity, and modern clothing meet naturally, beautifully, and unapologetically.**
